# Theory Master Map: From Boundary and First Beat to Life and Consciousness

## I. Ontological Stratification

### 1. The Boundary of Absolute Undefinedness

Undefined is only the terminal boundary role of zero positive ontological determination. It is not a “thing,” nor does it wait in time.

### 2. The Platonic Crystal

All statically describable forms, formulae, structures, complete trajectories, and repositories of snapshots may belong to the domain of static forms. Describability by itself does not produce actual occurrence.

### 3. The Domain of Actual Generation

The theory selects actually occurring, self-contained, rooted generative universes as its domain of inquiry. The First Beat is not the first second of physical time; it is the minimal structure of actual occurrence when there is no pre-given matter, time, or external executor.

## II. Root Structure

Jia Baolong Absolute Truth first gives the boundary proposition:

\[
U:\quad
\forall M\in\mathfrak D\,
\forall P\in\mathrm{Pos},\,
\neg P(b_M).
\]

Here, \(\mathfrak D\) is the admissible class of models that fulfils the specified task of explaining “from nothing to something”; \(b_M\) is the terminal root position of model \(M\); and \(\mathrm{Pos}\) is the set of positive ontological predicates.

This theorem excludes positive content at the root, but it does not itself produce actuality:

\[
T_U\not\models
\exists a,b\,
\operatorname{ActualStep}(a,b).
\]

Under additional conditions for an actualized model, the unique binary total map without a fixed point is the swap:

\[
D=\{Y,N\},\qquad
\sigma(Y)=N,\qquad
\sigma(N)=Y.
\]

\[
\operatorname{Fix}(\sigma)=\varnothing,
\qquad
\sigma^2=\operatorname{id}_D.
\]

The static formula for swapping is still not equivalent to actual occurrence. Only when an actual step, self-containment, and result re-entry are added does the system identify it as the actuality core of PR, or as sustained PR re-entry.

## III. PR–ER–LE

- **PR:** actual, non-fixed self-re-entry. It supplies persistent difference without being a classical contradiction explosion.
- **ER:** the Entity–Relation, or relational-configuration, layer. It preserves situations, adjacency, local structure, and patterns of possible interaction.
- **LE:** the Lazy Evaluation layer. It makes updating local, finite, truncated, selective, and history-dependent.
- **RULE:** the specific generative rule that determines how ER is updated and how LE operates. Without a specific RULE, PR–ER–LE is only an architecture and cannot uniquely derive this universe.

The dependency relation should be written as:

\[
\text{Conditions of the First Beat}
\Rightarrow
\text{the normal form of PR}
\Rightarrow
\text{the PR–ER–LE architecture}
\xRightarrow{\text{specific RULE}}
\text{possible chaos and upward emergence}.
\]

The final arrow is not a necessary implication.

## V. Key Revisions in the Development of the Theory

1. **Initial phase:** SR–ER–LE and medium-free logical networks were used to explain physical rendering.
2. **Development phase:** PR replaced the overly broad notion of self-reference, emphasizing non-fixed re-entry, topological relation, and lazy evaluation.
3. **Axiomatic phase:** Undefined, the static Platonic Crystal, and the dynamic generative tree were distinguished, and a two-axiom system was proposed.
4. **Computational phase:** TGP, SR-IC, graph processes, and phase-transition simulations were used to investigate chaos, proto-matter, and life-like emergence.
5. **Revision phase:** it was recognized that Undefined does not produce actuality, static forms cannot execute themselves, and any derivation of the First Beat must state the assumptions of its model class openly.
6. **Convergence phase:** the present rigorous version was formed through the absolute-truth boundary theorem, candidate classification, the binary logical razor, backward tracing, and atemporal co-arising.
7. **Application phase:** operational self-reference, integration, memory, and closed-loop regulation were applied to research on consciousness and AI alignment.

## VI. Current Research Frontier

The current frontier includes constructing specific RULEs; identifying stable structures from chaos to proto-matter; studying proto-matter interactions, proto-chemistry, replication, compartments, life organization, consciousness, and the correspondence between models and reality.
