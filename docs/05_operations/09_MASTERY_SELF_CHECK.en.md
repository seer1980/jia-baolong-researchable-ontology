# Theory Mastery Self-Check

Before claiming to have mastered this theory, one must independently answer the following questions. An answer missing a premise or boundary does not count as full mastery.

## A. Jia Baolong Axiom System

1. Why cannot Undefined, the Platonic Crystal, and the actual generative domain be regarded as three entities of the same kind?
2. What function does each of PR, ER, and LE provide, and why cannot specific RULE be omitted?
3. Which early axiomatic formulations were revised by the papers of July 2026?
4. Why is a negation, swap, or PR graph written on paper not equivalent to the First Beat of a universe?

## B. Jia Baolong Absolute Truth

5. Write \(U\) and explain \(\mathfrak D\), \(\mathrm{Pos}\), and \(b_M\).
6. Why is Undefined the name of a role rather than an object carrying truth?
7. Why is fundamental uniqueness calculated by logical equivalence class?
8. Why is \(99.999\ldots\%=100\%\) only an entrance for recognition rather than a proof?
9. In what five precise senses is it “absolute,” and why is it not a zero-ary logical premise?
10. Why can \(U\) not by itself entail an actual event?

## C. The Seven-Part Argument for the First Beat

11. Distinguish the argument from research domain, the non-causal argument for Undefined, elimination of the external executor, and candidate comparison.
12. What are the four total unary maps on a binary set?
13. What does label symmetry exclude, and what does the non-fixed condition then exclude?
14. Why does \(\sigma^2=\operatorname{id}\) both support persistent swapping and prove that swapping itself is not chaos?
15. Why is primitive motion an important second candidate, yet insufficient to derive a specific universe?
16. When PR is traced backward from matter-like chaos, which axioms perform the real work of uniqueness?
17. What does the vertical bar in \(U_*\mid\mathrm{PR}\) explicitly not mean?

## D. From Proto-Matter to the Single Cell

18. What operational properties does proto-matter require, and why can a persistent pattern still be only proto-proto-matter?
19. Distinguish TGP triangles, real particles, and nonliving chemistry in origin-of-life research.
20. What new functions are required from proto-chemistry to heredity?
21. What are the three minimum conditions of Darwinian selection?
22. Why does the appearance of selection still not equal the first cell?
23. Why must replication, metabolism, and compartmentalization be coupled within a reproducing unit?
24. Which contents belong to the mainstream origin-of-life framework, and which are the Jia Baolong system’s original mapping?

## E. Theoretical Development

25. How does Rule 979 connect PR–ER–LE to proto-matter structures?
26. What new mechanisms are needed from proto-matter to proto-chemistry?
27. How do replication, metabolism, and compartmentalization form the first cell?
28. How does consciousness become a researcher within the emergence chain and recognize PR in reverse?

## Minimum Requirements for a Passing Answer

A passing answer must:

- define Undefined, \(U\), PR, ER, LE, and RULE accurately;
- write the three core formulae for \(U\), \(\sigma\), and the fact that static encoding does not entail occurrence;
- reproduce the complete seven-part argument for the First Beat;
- explain TGP’s chain of proto-matter, proto-chemistry, heredity, selection, and the first cell;
- reconstruct PR in reverse from the position of consciousness and the researcher.
