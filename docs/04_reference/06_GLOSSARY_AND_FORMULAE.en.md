# Core Terms and Mathematical Expressions

## Undefined

The terminal boundary role of zero positive ontological determination. It is not an object, a cause, an empty state, or a potential world.

## Ordinary Nothing

A conceptual state that can still be assigned stable determinations such as “empty” or “there is no such thing.” It differs from Absolute Undefinedness, to which even these determinations do not apply.

## Platonic Crystal

The total domain of statically describable forms, including formulae, complete histories, and possible structures. A form can be available for use without actually occurring.

## Actual Occurrence

The ontological object selected by the system, expressed through ActualStep or an analogous relation. A relational formula in static language does not automatically instantiate a predicate of actual occurrence.

## The First Beat

The minimal structure of actual occurrence when there is no pre-given matter, physical time, or external actual executor. It is not physical time \(t=1\).

## PR

Paradox–Reference. In its current strict meaning, it is the minimal normal form that is self-contained, actually occurring, non-fixed, and permits result re-entry. It is not the simultaneous assertion of \(p\) and \(\neg p\) within the same fixed evaluation.

## ER

Entity–Relation. It denotes relational situations, adjacency networks, effective entity identity, local structure, and structural memory.

## LE

Lazy Evaluation. It denotes local, delayed, finite, on-demand, or truncated updating / evaluation. It is the implementation mechanism through which propagation scales and physical constants emerge.

## RULE

The specific update law operating on the ER state space. PR–ER–LE is the architecture; RULE determines whether chaos, attractors, and higher-level organization form.

## Historical Terminology in the English Archive

> **Editorial note for source-faithful reading:** the terminology developed across papers written at different stages. English translations preserve the wording used by each source rather than silently rewriting earlier papers into the latest vocabulary.

- **PR:** the current canonical expansion is **Paradox–Reference**. Earlier papers also use **Primitive Reflexivity** and **Paradoxical Recursion**. These expressions mark stages in the development and exposition of the same PR line; they should not be read as three independent present-day axioms.
- **LE:** the current canonical expansion is **Lazy Evaluation**. **Lazy Expansion** is retained where it appears in an earlier source and should be read in that source's historical context.
- **Trinity Theory / Trinity Topological Theory:** these are official historical proper names used in the relevant papers. They are not to be silently replaced by “Triadic Theory.”
- **Undefined / undefined / \(U\):** **Undefined** names the zero-positive ontological boundary; lowercase **undefined** may denote an ordinary programming-language value; \(U\) is the formal root proposition in the current specification. Context must not be discarded when these forms are indexed or compared.
