The theory thus extends from the root of existence to existence’s knowledge of itself, forming a complete structure of existence, generation, and cognition.

$$

\mathrm{Undefined}=\text{boundary role}.

“Even nothing is absent” is an intuitive expression for `Undefined`: at the ultimate root, positive determinations such as “something exists” and “there is a state named nothing” have not acquired a position. `Undefined` is the boundary of zero positive ontological determination.

$$
