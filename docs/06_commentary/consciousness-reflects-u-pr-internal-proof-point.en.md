## Abstract

“Consciousness reflecting on $U_*\mid\mathrm{PR}$” is not literary rhetoric at the end of Jia Baolong Researchable Ontology. It is the most important internal proof point of the entire existence–generation system. JBLRO begins with the root boundary $U_*$, which bears zero positive ontological determination, and the First Beat of actuality, PR. Through ER, LE, concrete RULE, chaos, proto-matter, life, and brain, it generates consciousness. Once consciousness has formed, it can trace this generative chain backward and recognize the structure of its own root.

When consciousness generated within a system can reconstruct that system’s first actual structure, generation and knowledge are no longer two unrelated lines. Ontology generates epistemology in consciousness; epistemology recognizes ontology in return. The self-referential capacity at the top and the self-reflexive structure at the bottom thereby close into one loop.
