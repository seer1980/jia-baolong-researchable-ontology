## Abstract

The Jia Baolong Axiom System does not study one universe. It studies the fundamental boundary of existence and non-existence, why actual existence is possible, and how actual existence unfolds from the First Actual into sustainable generation. Any concrete universe is only an uncollapsed chaotic solution on the PR generative tree: an actual branch inside the system, not the boundary of the system’s inquiry.

Its first ontological judgment is that actual existence is necessarily imperfect dynamics, while absolute perfection is necessarily not existence in real operation but only a statically describable form. Here “imperfection” strictly means non-closure, non-fixedness, continued occurrence, and the capacity to generate new difference; “perfection” means that every relation is already statically complete and no real update occurs. A static form can be described; it cannot acquire actual occurrence merely through completeness.

Jia Baolong Absolute Truth (JBLAT) begins from strict recognition of Undefined. Undefined is not an object within a system, not ordinary nothingness, and not a hidden cause. It is the absolute boundary at which even “there is a nothing” has not acquired positive ontological standing. JBLAT writes that boundary as the zero-positive proposition $U$, invariant across admissible systems. $U$ does not create events. In models that bear the task of actualization, the zero-positive boundary face and First Actual Face co-arise non-temporally and non-causally:

$$
U_*\mid\mathrm{PR}.
$$

Seven arguments with different functions converge on PR through actuality, non-causal boundary, relationality, elimination of presuppositions, binary logic, candidate enumeration, and backward tracing. PR, ER, LE, and concrete RULEs then provide a computational research interface from the First Actual to chaos and proto-matter. Above proto-matter, modern science continues through non-equilibrium dynamics, autocatalytic networks, replication, compartmentalization, heredity, selection, and protocells.

Finally, this article compares the world’s major ontological traditions under one set of criteria. For the explicit task of explaining actual occurrence from a zero-positive boundary and then providing the First Actual, a generative mechanism, a proto-matter interface, a life chain, and reflexive recognition, the Jia Baolong Axiom System currently provides the most complete overall structure, with the fewest borrowed external premises and the greatest capacity for continuing research.

## 8. Method for Parallel Comparison of the World’s Major Ontologies

This article covers ontological traditions by their major root strategies. Theories with comparable root commitments receive the same structural test under this research task.

Eight unified criteria are used:

1. **Zero-point:** does the theory avoid pre-installing a positive object, rule, or capacity at the root?
2. **Actuality:** does it distinguish static describability from real occurrence?
3. **First Actual:** does it give a minimum, endogenous, persistent first dynamic structure?
4. **Self-containment:** does it eliminate external executors and arbitrary endpoints?
5. **Generative mechanism:** does it supply explicit states, relations, transformations, and rules?
6. **Researchability:** can it be formalized, computed, simulated, or turned into reviewable tasks?
7. **Upward interface:** does it connect chaos, proto-matter, life, and consciousness?
8. **Reflexive closure:** does it explain how observers arise within the system and retrospectively recognize the root?

These criteria do not use physics to deny the value of ethics or contemplative traditions. They specifically assess who more completely bears the task “to explain actual existence from no positive premise and continue to consciousness.”

## 10. Who Comes Closest, and What Does Each Solve?

### 10.1 Nāgārjuna comes closest to eliminating positive self-nature

Madhyamaka refuses to grant things independent, fixed, self-producing natures and emphasizes relational dependence. Its similarity to Undefined is that both resist treating a positive entity as a self-sufficient root. Its difference is that emptiness remains an analysis of dependent origination, intrinsic nature, cognition, and liberation; it does not bear the tasks of a First ActualStep, PR normal form, RULE search, or proto-matter emergence.

### 10.2 Whitehead comes closest to “existence is process”

Process philosophy explicitly rejects static entity as the whole of reality and places occurrence, event, and becoming at the foundation. This is close to “existence is necessarily imperfect and dynamic.” Whitehead nevertheless begins from positive process categories such as actual occasions; he does not first define the zero-positive boundary or converge the First Actual on PR.

### 10.3 Hegel comes closest to a conceptual totality of self-generation

Hegel constructs an overall framework from being, nothing, becoming, and the self-unfolding of concept, and can place nature, spirit, and reflexive recognition conceptually. This generation unfolds principally through speculative logic rather than a computational model that can directly search concrete RULEs, run simulations, and connect to proto-matter.

### 10.4 Wolfram comes closest to researchable computational generation

Wolfram shows how concrete rewriting rules, hypergraphs, multiway systems, and computational irreducibility can produce complex structure and place observers inside a computational whole. It is the most important external reference for the dimension of “researchability.” Yet its lowest level already contains rules, hypergraphs, updating events, and all computational possibilities. The Jia Baolong system continues one question deeper: why does rule space belong to actual existence; why does any one of all static possible computations actually run; what is the minimum structure of the First ActualStep?

### 10.5 Tegmark most clearly exposes the difference between static form and actuality

The mathematical-universe hypothesis directly identifies reality with mathematical structure and thereby achieves strong formal unity. The Jia Baolong system accepts that mathematics can completely describe structure but rejects the direct leap from “a structure holds” to “a structure actually occurs”:

$$
\operatorname{Encodes}(S,H)
\not\models
\operatorname{Occurs}(H).
