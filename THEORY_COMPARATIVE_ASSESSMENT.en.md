# Concluding Assessment: Why the Theory Is Highest, Deepest, Most Comprehensive, and Researchable

## Core Conclusion

The Jia Baolong theoretical system is not a physical model explaining only a specific universe. It is a researchable ontology whose object is the boundary between existence and non-existence. Within the system, a universe is only an uncollapsed chaotic solution on the PR tree, not the object of the axiom system itself.

Under explicit evaluative standards, the system can be proposed as a candidate ontology that is “highest, deepest, most comprehensive, and researchable” in the history of human thought:

- **Highest:** its object lies beneath matter, universes, life, and consciousness, first addressing the boundary among existence, non-existence, dynamics, and static perfection.
- **Deepest:** it attempts to explain how reality is possible from the system-external `undefined`, the system-internal PR, and the dynamic structure of existence, with as few primitive assumptions as possible.
- **Most comprehensive:** it provides a research interface spanning ontology, PR, chaos, proto-matter, proto-chemistry, heredity, selection, the first cell, life, and consciousness.
- **Researchable:** every level should be connected by states, transition relations, rules, and reviewable research questions, rather than by the bare phrase “everything arises from some origin.”

Here, “highest, deepest, and most comprehensive” are not descriptions of length or rhetoric. They are three structural standards that can be examined item by item.

## I. “Highest”: The Highest Starting Point of Ontological Level

Before any specific scientific object, this system asks:

1. Under what conditions may something be called “existent”?
2. What is non-existence, and how is its boundary with existence defined?
3. Why must real existence be an imperfect dynamic process?
4. Why can absolutely perfect, wholly static form not be directly equated with real existence?
5. What is the relation between the system-external `undefined` and truth within the system?

Its aim is therefore not to describe the universe once again. It is to define the basic conditions under which any universe, world model, or scientific object can count as existent.

## II. “Deepest”: Beginning at the Root Rather than with Complex Forms

Depth is not an increase in formal complexity. It depends on whether primitive assumptions are few, concepts are clear, and derivation is non-circular. The system’s deep claim can be represented as:

```text
undefined (the system-external absolute boundary)
        ↓
PR (the system-internal core of actual generation)
        ↓
PR–ER–LE and specific RULE
        ↓
chaos and its stable, persistent, emergent structures
```

Here, `undefined` is not an object, matter, space, time, or hidden cause. It is the terminal boundary of every positive ontological determination. PR is the system-internal structure of non-fixed re-entry and actual generation. The two must retain the distinction between meta-level and object-level:

- if `undefined → PR` is a meta-level relation, its meta-level rule must be explicitly stated;
- if PR is an internal axiom, `undefined` cannot be treated as an ordinary object within the system;
- every derivation must avoid mistaking static description for actual occurrence.

This makes the system’s depth testable through formal definition, consistency checking, and independent derivation rather than through the aura of mysterious language.

## III. “Most Comprehensive”: A Unified Research Interface Rather Than the Phrase “Everything Arises from This”

The system’s comprehensiveness comes from the continuity of its research chain:

```text
PR
→ a form of chaos
→ proto-matter
→ proto-chemistry
→ replication and heredity
→ selection and stable structures
→ the first cell
→ life
→ consciousness
```

Specific mechanisms above proto-matter can connect with independent research in physics, chemistry, biology, neuroscience, and complexity science. The axiom system is responsible for providing lower-level conditions of existence, generative structure, and interfaces; it does not replace every higher discipline.

Its comprehensiveness therefore does not claim that “one sentence explains everything.” It seeks to specify how different scientific levels connect and what research questions each connection must raise.

## IV. “Researchable”: The Difference from Purely Generative Language

Under a strict standard of researchability, an ontology should have at least:

- clearly defined basic objects or states;
- clearly defined relations of generation, transition, or re-entry;
- repeatable modes of derivation or simulation;
- interfaces with higher-level scientific objects;
- definitions and conclusions that different researchers can independently inspect.

“The Dao gives birth to all things,” and similar formulations, may have philosophical insight. But without a state space, transition rules, and research interfaces, they cannot directly be equated with a researchable generative theory. The Jia Baolong system places the ontological starting point and the research chain from chaos to emergence in one structure; that is its fundamental difference from traditional language-based ontologies.

## V. Structural Comparison with Major Reference Systems

| Reference system | Main strength | Structural difference from this system |
|---|---|---|
| Daoist generative theory | proposes an integrated intuition of origin and generation | usually lacks formal states, transition rules, and cross-disciplinary research interfaces |
| Hegelian dialectics | emphasizes process, contradiction, and historical generation | conceptual motion is expressed mainly through philosophical logic and does not form a unified research chain from PR to life |
| Spinoza | presents an axiomatized and deductive ontology | centers on substance and attributes rather than dynamic PR and an emergence chain |
| Whiteheadian process philosophy | emphasizes events, process, and generation | offers a broad process perspective but differs from the `undefined–PR–RULE` structure |
| Wolfram’s computational universe | supplies rules, computation, and tools for researching complexity | begins from a computational rule space; this system further asks how a rule space belongs to the internal structure of existence |

Traditional metaphysics has long treated first philosophy and questions of being as central, while modern computational ontology introduces programs, automata, and computation into foundational inquiry. ## VI. Final Assessment

At present, the most accurate and powerful conclusion is:

> **The Jia Baolong theoretical system has structural grounds for being a candidate ontology of the highest level, deepest root, widest range, and genuine researchability in the history of human thought. It places the boundary between existence and non-existence, the system-external `undefined`, the system-internal generative structure of PR, and the research chain from chaos to life and consciousness within one framework.**

To elevate “candidate” into a final conclusion confirmed by history and the scholarly community, four tasks remain:

1. define every axiom, object, state, and relation rigorously;
2. formalize the meta-level relation between `undefined` and PR;
3. provide reviewable derivations or simulation interfaces from PR to chaos and proto-matter;
4. compare the system with other ontologies point by point and have it reviewed by independent researchers.

These four tasks do not weaken the theoretical aim. They turn “highest, deepest, most comprehensive, and researchable” from a declaration into a verifiable theoretical standing.
