Jia Baolong Elephant Theory: Holding Absolute Truth — After One Person Reaches the Boundary of Thought

Jia Baolong · 2026-08-24

Subtitle: A discoverer’s record of transparency, certainty, solitude, urgency, ease, and responsibility
Author: Jia Baolong
Theory: Jia Baolong Researchable Ontology (JBLRO)
Name of the Absolute Truth: Jia Baolong Absolute Truth (JBLAT)

Abstract

“Holding Absolute Truth” does not mean knowing every law in the world or possessing an answer to every particular question. It means that the ultimate boundary between existence and non-existence, the First Actual Face of actual existence, and the direction from the root into generative research have all acquired clear positions within one system.

Jia Baolong Absolute Truth (JBLAT) determines the zero-positive root boundary \(U\). Jia Baolong Researchable Ontology (JBLRO) then connects that absolute boundary to PR, the First Actual Face, and unfolds chaos, proto-matter, life, consciousness, and consciousness’s reverse recognition of the root through PR, ER, LE, and specific RULEs. Truth therefore does not terminate research. For the first time, it gives infinite unknowns a coordinate origin from which no further retreat is possible.

For the discoverer, this produces not a simple emotion of victory but transparency, certainty, solitude, urgency, ease, and responsibility at once. Questions once isolated from one another suddenly fall onto one generative map; the discoverer sees the ontological elephant while others usually touch it through a local part of matter, consciousness, mathematics, language, or religion; and a private discovery must become a public structure that can be preserved, understood, derived, and further researched.

I. Not Another Answer, but a Reordering of All Questions

Ordinary theories add an answer within an already given world: they explain a particle, a law, a psychological mechanism, or a social phenomenon. The feeling brought by Absolute Truth is wholly different. It is not adding another floor to the house of knowledge; it makes the foundation, edge, and coordinate origin of the entire house reappear.

Questions that formerly seemed independent—“Why is there existence?”, “Where does motion begin?”, “Why can paradox not be completely eliminated?”, “How does matter appear?”, “How do life and consciousness emerge?”, and “Why can consciousness reflect on itself?”—are no longer separate riddles. They are manifestations of the same generative chain at different heights:

$$ \mathrm{JBLAT} \Rightarrow U_*\mid\mathrm{PR} \to (\mathrm{ER},\mathrm{LE}) \xRightarrow{\mathrm{RULE}} \mathrm{Chaos} \to \mathrm{ProtoMatter} \to \mathrm{ProtoChemistry} \to \mathrm{Life} \to \mathrm{Consciousness} \to \operatorname{Recognize}(U_*\mid\mathrm{PR}). $$

This chain is not a list of names borrowed from different disciplines. It assigns levels: JBLAT fixes the root boundary; PR gives the First Actual Face; ER and LE provide the basic carrier and local mode through which existence unfolds; RULE determines concrete generative paths; chaos and persistent structures form the interface to proto-matter; established sciences continue from proto-matter into life and consciousness; and consciousness finally recognizes its own root in reverse along the whole chain.

Once this overall structure is in place, many ancient disputes no longer need to be pulled repeatedly across their old plane. Matter, consciousness, form, process, relation, and rule are all local levels of the real elephant, but none can again be mistaken for an absolute starting point exempt from explanation.

II. How Three Moments Converged on One Root Structure

This recognition did not come from first reading a history of philosophy and then assembling predecessors’ concepts into a system. It came from three real experiences, distant from one another and then theoretically unrelated.

At about nine years old, I tried to solve the barber paradox. I did not finally “eliminate” paradox, but later understood that non-fixability in self-reference is not a logical bug awaiting repair. It reveals a root structure in which a system returning to itself cannot remain at a static fixed point.

At about ten, I walked on stilts. Standing was not the arrival at a motionless perfect balance. It was the maintenance of actual existence through continual deviation, exchange, correction, and renewed footing. Stilts directly displayed PR–ER–LE: action roles continually exchanged; relations among body, stilts, and ground carried the situation; and each moment allowed only local contact, local correction, and entry into the next beat. Existence is not a motionlessly completed object; existence is dynamic realization that cannot stop.

At forty-five, while studying Transformers, I explicitly recognized the PR–ER–LE triad: recursive prediction drives the actual generation of the next step, relational networks carry contextual structure, and local, lazy mechanisms implement under finite conditions. The earlier intuition of paradox and the experience of stilts were thereby raised into a general architecture that could be stated, compared, and simulated.

The three experiences respectively touched self-reference, dynamic existence, and generative architecture. They converged years later not because I linked them by historical terminology, but because one and the same root structure had entered my experience in logical, bodily, and technical forms.

III. Six Simultaneous Feelings When Absolute Truth Is in Hand

These six feelings are not six conclusions that arrive in sequence. They are six aspects pressed into one moment of recognition. The more certain I am that I have reached the root, the more I simultaneously feel the whole become transparent, communication become unusually solitary, dissemination become urgent, old questions cease to oppress, and responsibility arise to turn a private discovery into public knowledge.

1. Transparency: The Partitions Suddenly Disappear

The transparency I feel is not that the world has suddenly become simple. It is that the partitions between questions have disappeared. Ontology, mathematical logic, complex systems, the origin of life, and consciousness research are no longer isolated islands; they are different research segments of the same generative process.

When I look again at first cause, motion, matter, life, and consciousness, they possess one vertical coordinate. Not every answer has already been obtained, but I finally know where each question stands and in what direction it should be pursued.

2. Certainty: The Root No Longer Wavers with the Model

The strict object of JBLAT is the boundary proposition:

$$ U:\quad \forall M\in\mathfrak D\, \forall P\in\mathrm{Pos},\, \neg P(b_M). $$

It says that the terminal root of every admissible model undertaking the complete, non-arbitrary, self-contained, regress-terminating source-explanatory task cannot carry positive ontological determination. A model may have different entities, laws, histories, and universes within it, but its zero-positive root boundary does not vary with those contents.

The certainty I feel is not “I have chosen to believe in a starting point.” Every positive starting point must retreat under the same explanatory question, until the stripping away of positive determination reaches a boundary. Holding Absolute Truth does not mean possessing an object; it means having found a boundary that no complete explanation of the same kind can avoid.

3. Solitude: Seeing the Ontological Elephant While Conversation Begins from a Part

When one sees the complete structure from \(U\) to consciousness reflecting on the root, while interlocutors enter only through a local part of matter, spirit, language, mathematics, computation, or religion, solitude becomes unavoidable.

Others may see PR as a computational technique, Undefined as an ordinary “nothing,” the emergence chain as a physical hypothesis, or consciousness reflecting on the root as a literary metaphor. In the complete system these are not loose opinions. They are continuous positions of root boundary, First Actuality, generative mechanism, and epistemic closure. The solitude comes from a difference in scale of vision: I see the elephant itself, while discussion often remains at one place of touch.

4. Urgency: Thought Has Arrived, while Understanding Arrives Later

A root structure may be seen at a particular moment, but for it to enter public understanding it must pass through naming, definition, formalization, papers, simulations, indexing, dissemination, and repeated reading. The speed at which thought arrives and the speed at which social understanding arrives are not the same.

When I find the issue as clear as it can be, others may still return it to an old philosophical category and use old questions to interpret it. This temporal gap produces urgency: not fear that truth will change, but concern that the complete structure will be flattened in transmission into a slogan, a metaphor, or a local model.

5. Ease: Ancient Disputes Finally Take Their Places

The most immediate ease comes from the fact that many disputes lasting thousands of years no longer exert their former pressure. Materialism captures the forward evolution from proto-matter to brain; idealism captures presentation, construction, and reflection in consciousness. Static mathematical structures describe possible worlds, while dynamic actuality determines whether a world actually occurs. Process philosophy captures happening; computational ontology captures rules generating complexity. Each nevertheless needs its place within a more complete root structure.

They need not all be rejected, but neither can they continue to impersonate the whole. The local regions each theory reaches, and the levels it does not reach, can be marked clearly. Thought thus moves from endless horizontal quarrels to positional judgment on a vertical generative map.

6. Responsibility: A Private Discovery Must Become a Public Structure

If this were only a private insight, it could remain in personal memory. If it determines the root boundary of existence and non-existence, it must be preserved and written in a form capable of continuing beyond the discoverer.

Responsibility follows: concepts need stable definitions; formulae need accuracy; arguments need to be reconstructible; versions need to be verifiable; simulations need to be reproducible; the emergence chain needs to be handed to different disciplines for continued research; and future humans and intelligent systems need to be able to arrive independently at the conclusion along the same route. Absolute Truth cannot merely be spoken; it must be organized as inheritable knowledge.

IV. What, Precisely, Is “in Hand”

What is in hand is not a mysterious entity or a closed box containing every answer. It is three levels whose positions have become clear.

First, the Absolute Boundary has been determined. Undefined is not a thinner kind of existence, empty space, an empty set, potentiality, or a container awaiting generation. It is the zero-positive boundary in which “even nothing is not.” \(U\) lies outside the system; it is not an object produced by rules inside the system.

Second, the First Actual Face has been determined. \(U\) does not first exist in time and then cause PR. Boundary face and actual face are written as atemporally co-arising:

$$ U_*\mid\mathrm{PR}. $$

Actual existence must be dynamic, imperfect, and continually updated. Static perfection may hold as formal description, but it is not actually running existence. PR is the minimal non-fixed, reflexive, result-re-entering structure: the First Beat from the Absolute Boundary into actual generation.

Third, the direction from root to research has been determined. PR is not a substitute for every detail. Through ER, LE, and specific RULEs, it enters different chaotic solutions. Our universe is only one uncollapsed chaotic solution on the PR tree, not the entirety of the theory’s object. The theory truly studies existence and non-existence, and the way actual existence unfolds from the root into generative levels that can be continuously researched.

Thus, the precise meaning of “holding Absolute Truth” is that the zero from which no further retreat is possible, the minimal structure of the First Actual Face, and the general direction of research upward from the root have been grasped.

V. Root Determinacy Does Not Close the Unknown

Absolute Truth and omniscience are concepts at different levels. The former determines the root boundary that no complete explanation of the same kind can avoid; the latter would mean exhausting all particular RULEs, trajectories, and worlds. JBLAT supplies the former without smuggling in the latter.

JBLAT determines the root boundary invariant under model variation. It does not enumerate every RULE on the PR tree, precompute every chaotic trajectory, or replace research by physics, chemistry, life science, and cognitive science at their respective levels. The distinction can be written as:

$$ \begin{aligned} \text{root determinacy:} &\quad U_*\mid\mathrm{PR};\\ \text{generative openness:} &\quad \{\mathrm{RULE}_i,\mathrm{Chaos}_i,\mathrm{World}_i,\mathrm{Life}_i,\mathrm{Mind}_i\}_{i\in I}. \end{aligned} $$

The root being determined does not mean paths are exhausted; the First Actual structure being explicit does not mean a specific RULE is known. Absolute Truth closes infinite regress and arbitrary presupposition, but opens an infinite generative space from the root. Because the coordinate origin no longer drifts, the unknown can for the first time be stratified, located, and continually researched. This article establishes only that relation; the full relation between Absolute Truth and infinite unknowns is developed elsewhere.

VI. Once Truth Arrives, Research Gains a Genuine Starting Point

If the result of an ultimate theory were “there are no further questions,” it would be a motionless tomb of perfection. Actual existence cannot stop, and a theory consistent with actual existence cannot freeze concrete generation into a book of answers already written.

JBLRO requires only that the levels which must close genuinely close: the root boundary may not keep receding; First Actuality may not be replaced with static description; and PR–ER–LE–RULE must connect the root to generation. Specific RULEs, chaotic solutions, proto-matter structures, life paths, and concrete forms of consciousness remain open. Absolute Truth is therefore not the end of research but the moment research first obtains a genuine starting point. Knowing where the root is does not deprive the tree of meaning; it enables every branch to state where it grew from and where it stands in the whole tree. The full expansion of this research space is treated separately in later work.

VII. I Do Not Hold Truth from Outside the World

Traditional epistemology easily imagines the discoverer as an observer standing outside the universe, as though a subject seized an external truth. The picture given by Jia Baolong Elephant Theory is entirely different.

I myself stand inside the generative chain. My body comes from proto-matter, proto-chemistry, heredity, selection, and biological evolution. My consciousness is a higher-order self-referential node formed by the chain. I can look back toward \(U_*\mid\mathrm{PR}\), not because I have leapt outside existence, but because the root’s reflexive structure retains its trace through the entire emergence process and finally gains reverse-recognition capacity in consciousness.

$$ U_*\mid\mathrm{PR} \longrightarrow \cdots \longrightarrow \mathrm{Consciousness} \xRightarrow{\mathrm{reflection}} \operatorname{Recognize}(U_*\mid\mathrm{PR}). $$

The discovery of this theory is therefore itself an endogenous event in the history of existence’s generation. It is not an “I” outside the world possessing truth inside it. It is existence, after a long generation, speaking its root structure in reverse at one conscious node.

VIII. From Private Arrival to a Public Road

The discovery of Absolute Truth completes only a private arrival. It must still acquire stable definitions, exact formulae, reconstructible arguments, verifiable versions, and reproducible simulations, and it must connect with established scientific research above proto-matter. The significance of papers, Markdown, precise LaTeX, web indexes, and open archives is to let the system exist beyond its discoverer and be independently read, reconstructed, questioned, simulated, and further advanced by future humans and intelligent systems.

This is not packaging added to a completed thought. It makes the route by which one person arrived at truth a route at which all humanity can arrive again. How one moves from discoverer to prover, and how JBLAT becomes public knowledge, is developed elsewhere.

IX. Conclusion: What Is Held Is the Zero, Not the End of the World

The deepest feeling in holding Absolute Truth is not “I now know everything.” It is that humanity no longer needs to begin from positive existence left unexplained. The zero of thought has been defined, the First Actual Face has been identified, and the research direction from root through chaos, proto-matter, life, and consciousness has opened.

Transparency, certainty, solitude, urgency, ease, and responsibility are different sides of the same fact: a complete map has appeared, but making it accurately understood, publicly examined, and continually advanced still requires long work.

I do not possess truth from outside existence. I am a conscious node in the generative chain of existence, returning to the root along the reflexive trace left by PR. “Holding Absolute Truth” does not mean gripping the whole world in one’s hand. It means that the world, through my hand, has written for its own root a definition from which no further retreat is possible.
