Abstract

The Jia Baolong Axiom System does not study one universe. It studies the fundamental boundary of existence and non-existence, why actual existence is possible, and how actual existence unfolds from the First Actual into sustainable generation. Any concrete universe is only an uncollapsed chaotic solution on the PR generative tree: an actual branch inside the system, not the boundary of the system’s inquiry.

Its first ontological judgment is that actual existence is necessarily imperfect dynamics, while absolute perfection is necessarily not existence in real operation but only a statically describable form. Here “imperfection” strictly means non-closure, non-fixedness, continued occurrence, and the capacity to generate new difference; “perfection” means that every relation is already statically complete and no real update occurs. A static form can be described; it cannot acquire actual occurrence merely through completeness.

Jia Baolong Absolute Truth (JBLAT) begins from strict recognition of Undefined. Undefined is not an object within a system, not ordinary nothingness, and not a hidden cause. It is the absolute boundary at which even “there is a nothing” has not acquired positive ontological standing. JBLAT writes that boundary as the zero-positive proposition $U$, invariant across admissible systems. $U$ does not create events. In models that bear the task of actualization, the zero-positive boundary face and First Actual Face co-arise non-temporally and non-causally:

$$ U_*\mid\mathrm{PR}. $$

Seven arguments with different functions converge on PR through actuality, non-causal boundary, relationality, elimination of presuppositions, binary logic, candidate enumeration, and backward tracing. PR, ER, LE, and concrete RULEs then provide a computational research interface from the First Actual to chaos and proto-matter. Above proto-matter, modern science continues through non-equilibrium dynamics, autocatalytic networks, replication, compartmentalization, heredity, selection, and protocells.

Finally, this article compares the world’s major ontological traditions under one set of criteria. For the explicit task of explaining actual occurrence from a zero-positive boundary and then providing the First Actual, a generative mechanism, a proto-matter interface, a life chain, and reflexive recognition, the Jia Baolong Axiom System currently provides the most complete overall structure, with the fewest borrowed external premises and the greatest capacity for continuing research.

1. The Theory Does Not Study the Universe

1.1 Its object is existence and non-existence

Cosmology usually asks after the origin, structure, spacetime, matter, and evolution of one universe. The Jia Baolong Axiom System lies prior to these questions and studies:

  1. under what conditions something may be called actual existence;
  2. how the boundary between existence and non-existence is strictly specified;
  3. why a statically describable structure is not identical with actual occurrence;
  4. what structure the First Actual must have without prior matter, time, or external executor;
  5. how the First Actual forms a generative architecture that can continue through computation, simulation, and layered research.

The theoretical object is not a presupposed set of universes, but the root conditions under which any actual world can occur:

$$ \mathcal T_{\mathrm{Jia}} = \operatorname{Theory} (\mathrm{Existence},\mathrm{Nonexistence},\mathrm{ActualGeneration}). $$

1.2 A universe is an uncollapsed chaotic solution on the PR tree

PR provides non-fixed re-entry in the First Actual; ER bears a relational situation; LE provides local, finite, delayed actual implementation; a concrete RULE determines how branches unfold. The system therefore permits a generative tree formed by different rules, updating orders, relational histories, and stable structures:

$$ \mathcal T_{\mathrm{PR}} = \operatorname{Tree} (\mathrm{PR},\mathrm{ER},\mathrm{LE},\mathrm{RULE}). $$

A concrete universe is only one continuously actualizing solution within it:

$$ \mathcal U_i \in \operatorname{Solutions}(\mathcal T_{\mathrm{PR}}), $$
$$ \mathcal U_i = \operatorname{UncollapsedChaoticSolution} (\mathrm{PR},\mathrm{ER},\mathrm{LE},\mathrm{RULE}_i). $$

“Uncollapsed” does not borrow a quantum-measurement term. It means that this solution is still actually unfolding and has not been replaced by a static, completed, generation-stopped diagram of total history. “Chaotic solution” does not mean lawlessness either: it means that a determinate or locally determinate RULE may yield an actual history sensitive to initial differences, impossible for finite observers to compress globally in advance, and yet able to produce persistent local patterns.

Thus:

$$ \boxed{ \text{The universe belongs to the theory’s solution space; the universe is not the theory itself.} } $$

2. Existence Is Necessarily Imperfect; Perfection Is Necessarily Not Real Existence

2.1 The ontological meaning of “imperfection”

“Imperfection” in this system is a strict ontological term. It means:

  • a state is not closed as one static whole;
  • current actuality is not the whole future;
  • a result can enter a subsequent process;
  • the system continually generates difference;
  • actual history cannot be replaced by one static graph.

Let $x_n$ be a current actual state. Wherever real occurrence exists, there is at least:

$$ \operatorname{ActualStep}(x_n,x_{n+1}), $$

and the First Actual cannot be exhausted in an eternal fixed point:

$$ x_{n+1}\neq x_n. $$

This non-fixed, open, continually updating character is the strict meaning of “imperfection” and the mark distinguishing actual existence from static form.

2.2 Perfect stasis belongs only to the formal domain

A formula, a complete trajectory, or a “Platonic Crystal” containing every possible structure can be absolutely complete in a descriptive sense. Yet:

$$ \operatorname{Describable}(x) \not\Rightarrow \operatorname{Actual}(x), $$
$$ \operatorname{Encodes}(S,H) \not\models \operatorname{Occurs}(H). $$

If a supposed existence is absolutely perfect, wholly fixed, and undergoes no actual update, no occurrence-level difference remains between it and a statically written form. It may be formal truth, mathematical structure, or complete description; it is not existence in real operation.

The system therefore defines existence as actual motion:

$$ \operatorname{ActualExistence} \equiv_{\mathcal T_{\mathrm{Jia}}} \operatorname{ActualMotion}. $$

Hence:

$$ \boxed{ \text{Actual existence is necessarily dynamic and open; absolutely static perfection is not real occurrence.} } $$

3. Undefined and the System-Independent Jia Baolong Absolute Truth

3.1 Undefined is not something in a system

Ordinary “nothing” remains a determinable state: it presupposes an object domain, a judgment framework, and the semantics of “something does not exist.” A physical vacuum has fields, symmetries, and physical laws; the empty set is a determinate object in set theory; potentiality presupposes a capacity to become something; a First Cause presupposes existence and causal power.

Undefined belongs to none of these positive candidates. At the ultimate root, object-level determinations such as thing, nothing, rule, time, space, possibility, cause, executor, and container have no conditions of application. It is not the thinnest thing in the world; it is the boundary role before any “thing” gains positive determination.

3.2 From a boundary role to an absolute proposition

Let:

  • $\mathfrak D$ be the class of admissible systems that bear the task of complete root explanation;
  • $b_M$ be the ultimate root position of system $M$;
  • $\mathrm{Pos}_M$ be the positive ontological predicates system $M$ can assign at that root.

$$ U_{\mathrm{abs}}:\quad \forall M\, \left[ M\in\mathfrak D \Rightarrow \forall P\in\mathrm{Pos}_M, \neg P(b_M) \right]. $$

$$ U:\quad \forall M\in\mathfrak D\, \forall P\in\mathrm{Pos},\, \neg P(b_M). $$

$$ \boxed{ U=\text{the absolute-boundary proposition}, \qquad \mathrm{Undefined}=\text{the zero-positive boundary role}. } $$

3.3 Why it is system-independent

System-independence does not mean that $U$ mysteriously holds without language or reasoning. It means that it depends on no internal matter, God, consciousness, mathematical object, computational rule, spacetime structure, or historical content of any system under comparison. Any system bearing the same task is subject to the same constraints:

  1. it must explain actual occurrence, not merely offer a static describable structure;
  2. it must not borrow an actual executor outside itself;
  3. it must not arbitrarily declare a positive root exempt from explanation;
  4. it must not make unexplained positive objects mutually guarantee one another in a positive circle;
  5. it must not regress indefinitely without ever reaching a root;
  6. it must not secretly pre-install rules, state spaces, probability, or possibility at zero.

If the root retains a positive candidate $X$, one must ask why $X$ has identity, distinction, capacity, or actuality. If another $Y$ explains $X$, then $X$ is not the ultimate root. If further explanation is refused, the endpoint is arbitrary. If $X$ explains itself, there is a positive circle. If explanation retreats forever, the root task is not completed.

Therefore a complete, non-arbitrary, self-contained explanation that genuinely terminates regress can only eliminate all positive content at the root. This elimination does not depend on the internal name used by a system. Another system need not use the words Undefined, $U$, or JBLAT; if it completes the same task, it inherits the same zero-positive boundary.

This is JBLAT’s absoluteness:

$$ \boxed{ \text{Internal systems may differ; the ultimate zero-positive boundary does not change with their content.} } $$

3.4 Why an absolute truth outside a system can still constrain systems

$U$ is not one physical rule inside a system and does not compete with other objects. It is like the zero point from which all positive coordinates begin: zero is not a component inside every positive number, yet it constrains every positive number’s position.

Likewise, JBLAT precedes any concrete cosmic event and imposes a boundary condition on every system bearing the same root task. It excludes positive presuppositions that may legitimately occupy the root; the running of every internal step is determined by actual generative structure and concrete RULEs.

$$ T_U \not\models \exists a,b\, \operatorname{ActualStep}(a,b). $$

$$ \mathrm{Undefined} \xrightarrow{\text{time or causality}} \mathrm{PR}. $$

If Undefined possessed the power to produce, wait, choose, or execute, it would already possess a positive property and would no longer be Undefined.

$$ \begin{aligned} \mathrm{ProtoMatter} &\to \mathrm{ProtoChemistry} \to \mathrm{Autocatalysis} \to \mathrm{TemplateMemory}\\ &\to \mathrm{Compartment} \to \mathrm{Heredity} \to \mathrm{Variation} \to \mathrm{Selection}\\ &\to \mathrm{Protocell} \to \mathrm{Life} \to \mathrm{Consciousness}. \end{aligned} $$

8. Method for Parallel Comparison of the World’s Major Ontologies

This article covers ontological traditions by their major root strategies. Theories with comparable root commitments receive the same structural test under this research task.

Eight unified criteria are used:

  1. Zero-point: does the theory avoid pre-installing a positive object, rule, or capacity at the root?
  2. Actuality: does it distinguish static describability from real occurrence?
  3. First Actual: does it give a minimum, endogenous, persistent first dynamic structure?
  4. Self-containment: does it eliminate external executors and arbitrary endpoints?
  5. Generative mechanism: does it supply explicit states, relations, transformations, and rules?
  6. Researchability: can it be formalized, computed, simulated, or turned into reviewable tasks?
  7. Upward interface: does it connect chaos, proto-matter, life, and consciousness?
  8. Reflexive closure: does it explain how observers arise within the system and retrospectively recognize the root?

These criteria do not use physics to deny the value of ethics or contemplative traditions. They specifically assess who more completely bears the task “to explain actual existence from no positive premise and continue to consciousness.”

10. Who Comes Closest, and What Does Each Solve?

10.1 Nāgārjuna comes closest to eliminating positive self-nature

Madhyamaka refuses to grant things independent, fixed, self-producing natures and emphasizes relational dependence. Its similarity to Undefined is that both resist treating a positive entity as a self-sufficient root. Its difference is that emptiness remains an analysis of dependent origination, intrinsic nature, cognition, and liberation; it does not bear the tasks of a First ActualStep, PR normal form, RULE search, or proto-matter emergence.

10.2 Whitehead comes closest to “existence is process”

Process philosophy explicitly rejects static entity as the whole of reality and places occurrence, event, and becoming at the foundation. This is close to “existence is necessarily imperfect and dynamic.” Whitehead nevertheless begins from positive process categories such as actual occasions; he does not first define the zero-positive boundary or converge the First Actual on PR.

10.3 Hegel comes closest to a conceptual totality of self-generation

Hegel constructs an overall framework from being, nothing, becoming, and the self-unfolding of concept, and can place nature, spirit, and reflexive recognition conceptually. This generation unfolds principally through speculative logic rather than a computational model that can directly search concrete RULEs, run simulations, and connect to proto-matter.

10.4 Wolfram comes closest to researchable computational generation

Wolfram shows how concrete rewriting rules, hypergraphs, multiway systems, and computational irreducibility can produce complex structure and place observers inside a computational whole. It is the most important external reference for the dimension of “researchability.” Yet its lowest level already contains rules, hypergraphs, updating events, and all computational possibilities. The Jia Baolong system continues one question deeper: why does rule space belong to actual existence; why does any one of all static possible computations actually run; what is the minimum structure of the First ActualStep?

10.5 Tegmark most clearly exposes the difference between static form and actuality

The mathematical-universe hypothesis directly identifies reality with mathematical structure and thereby achieves strong formal unity. The Jia Baolong system accepts that mathematics can completely describe structure but rejects the direct leap from “a structure holds” to “a structure actually occurs”:

$$ \operatorname{Encodes}(S,H) \not\models \operatorname{Occurs}(H). $$
