Jia Baolong Elephant Theory: An Integrated Understanding of the General Theory of Existence and Generation

Jia Baolong · 2026-08-11

Author: Jia Baolong
Theory: Jia Baolong Elephant Theory

Abstract

The Jia Baolong Elephant Theory studies the fundamental boundary of existence and nonexistence, how actuality begins, and how actual existence emerges along a continuous generative chain into chaos, proto-matter, life, consciousness, and backward recognition of the root. A universe is one uncollapsed chaotic solution on the PR tree; the theory’s object is broader than any particular universe.

The root is the zero-positive boundary proposition $U$, fixed by Jia Baolong Absolute Truth (JBLAT). In an actualization model, the Undefined boundary and PR’s First Beat form the boundary face and actual face of one root:

$$ U_*\mid\mathrm{PR}. $$

This root structure is the cause-end of the generative chain. PR, ER, LE, and RULE unfold into chaos, proto-matter, proto-chemistry, life, and consciousness. When consciousness forms formal systems capable of encoding and proving themselves, nonclosure at the root appears at the result-end as Gödelian incompleteness. The theory thus extends from the root of existence to existence’s knowledge of itself, forming a complete structure of existence, generation, and cognition.

The Jia Baolong Elephant Theory asks:

  1. What is the edge between existence and nonexistence?

  3. What minimal structure does the First Beat have?
  4. How does first actuality become a sustainable generative mechanism?
  5. How does that mechanism enter chaos, proto-matter, life, and consciousness?
  6. How does consciousness trace the full emergence chain backward to recognize PR and the root boundary?

Its complete structure is:

$$ \underbrace{U_*\mid\mathrm{PR}}_{\text{root and first actuality}} \to \mathrm{ER+LE} \xRightarrow{\mathrm{RULE}} \mathrm{Chaos} \to \mathrm{ProtoMatter} \to \mathrm{ProtoChemistry} \to \mathrm{Heredity} \to \mathrm{Selection} \to \mathrm{Protocell} \to \mathrm{Life} \to \mathrm{Consciousness} \to \mathrm{SelfReference}. $$

This is a general theory of existence and generation. Fundamental ontology specifies the root; formal logic specifies the boundary and First Beat; theoretical computer science and complexity science study generative rules; origin-of-life science studies mechanisms from proto-matter to cell; and consciousness science studies how systems form reflexive knowledge.

2. Jia Baolong Absolute Truth (JBLAT)

2.1 $U$ is a boundary proposition

Let $\mathfrak D$ be a nonempty admissible class of models that bears the full explanatory task from nothing to something; let $\mathrm{Pos}$ be a nonempty set of positive ontological predicates; and let $b_M$ be the ultimate root position of model $M$. Define:

$$ U:\quad \forall M\in\mathfrak D\, \forall P\in\mathrm{Pos},\, \neg P(b_M). $$

$U$ is a boundary proposition that can be true or false. Undefined is the zero-positive boundary role determined by $U$:

$$ \boxed{ U=\text{boundary proposition}, \qquad \mathrm{Undefined}=\text{boundary role}. } $$

“Even nothing is absent” is an intuitive expression for Undefined: at the ultimate root, positive determinations such as “something exists” and “there is a state named nothing” have not acquired a position. Undefined is the boundary of zero positive ontological determination.

2.2 The strict form of JBLAT

$$ \operatorname{JBLAT}(\mathfrak D,L_{\mathrm{root}}) \;:\Longleftrightarrow\; U\ \land\ \forall T\in\operatorname{Inv}(\mathfrak D,L_{\mathrm{root}}),\, U\models T. $$

This definition includes boundary truth and root uniqueness:

  1. $U$ remains invariant across all admissible models;
  2. changes in entities, laws, structures, and histories internal to a model preserve $U$;
  3. $U$ fixes every positive atom in root language to false;
  4. $U$ entails every root invariant in root language;
  5. every other fundamental generative proposition $G$ satisfies $G\leftrightarrow U$.

Thus, by logical equivalence class, $U$ is the sole fundamental absolute truth for this research task.

2.3 The epistemic form of the infinitely recurring percentage

$$ 99.999\ldots\%=100\%. $$

This equality reveals JBLAT’s epistemic structure: a boundary apparently open because it has no positive completed object logically fixes every root assignment completely. It is the recognition-form of absolute truth; explanatory closure, elimination of positive content, equivalence across model boundaries, and root uniqueness form the proof structure.

3. $U$ and PR: the cause-end of one root

The boundary proposition $U$ and PR stand in an atemporal co-arising relation:

$$ \operatorname{ActualFace}_{\mathcal M}(U_*) \cong \mathrm{PR}, $$
$$ U_*\mid\mathrm{PR}. $$

The bar denotes two aspects of one root determination:

  • $U_*$ is the boundary face of zero positive determination;
  • PR is the actual face after entry into actual occurrence.

The master map therefore uses $U_*\mid\mathrm{PR}$ rather than drawing $U$ and PR as two objects existing successively in time. Causal direction unfolds outward from this complete root structure:

$$ \underbrace{U_*\mid\mathrm{PR}}_{\text{cause-end}} \Longrightarrow \mathrm{ActualGeneration} \Longrightarrow \mathrm{EmergentStructure}. $$

$U$ retains the type of boundary proposition; PR provides the first dynamic of actual, fixed-point-free, self-reflexive, continuing generation. Together at the same root they form the cause-end of all later ER, LE, RULE, chaos, and emergence.

4. Seven arguments for the PR First Beat

PR is the minimal normal form of the First Beat: fixed-point-free, self-contained, and capable of result re-entry. Seven arguments establish distinct aspects of it.

4.1 Research domain and actuality

The theory studies actual occurrence:

$$ \operatorname{Describable}(x) \not\Rightarrow \operatorname{Actual}(x), $$
$$ \operatorname{Encodes}(S,H) \not\models \operatorname{Occurs}(H). $$

Static formulae, complete trajectories, ALL, and snapshot libraries are describable structures. Actuality is expressed by a genuinely occurring ActualStep.

4.2 The Undefined boundary

Undefined provides the zero-positive root boundary, while PR expresses the actual face. Their atemporal co-arising eliminates an external executor, prior matter, and prior physical time.

4.3 Relationality and internal adjacency

First actuality is supplied by a relation in occurrence:

$$ \operatorname{ActualStep}_W(a_0,a_1). $$

The result position participates in the next actual step, so occurrence continues within the system.

4.4 Stripping root presuppositions

External executors, preloaded matter, physical time, probability distributions, state libraries, and positive RULEs all become new objects to be questioned at the root. JBLAT’s explanatory closure converges the root onto zero positive determination.

4.5 The finite binary logical razor

Let

$$ D=\{Y,N\}. $$

The total unary maps on a binary set are identity, exchange, and two constant maps. Label symmetry retains identity and exchange; the no-fixed-point condition leaves only exchange:

$$ \mathfrak R(D^D)=\{\sigma\}, $$
$$ \sigma(Y)=N, \qquad \sigma(N)=Y, \qquad \operatorname{Fix}(\sigma)=\varnothing, \qquad \sigma^2=\operatorname{id}_D. $$

The actual running step is:

$$ \operatorname{ActualStep}_W(x_n,x_{n+1}) \land x_{n+1}=\sigma(x_n). $$

4.6 Exhaustion of first-dynamic candidates

A static complete structure provides description; a single flash lacks persistence; an external executor moves the root outside; a random first transition introduces sample space, measure, and actual sampling; affirmative self-generation introduces a seed, copying, and persistence rules. In the explicit minimal model-domain, PR unifies fixed-point-freedom, re-entry, and continuing actuality at the lowest structural cost.

4.7 Backward tracing and atemporal co-arising

Tracing backward from a formed chaotic history of proto-matter, the First Beat is located as the PR normal form under binary, unbiased, fixed-point-free, and continuing conditions. Forward root analysis and backward emergence tracing meet at $U_*\mid\mathrm{PR}$.

Together the seven arguments establish:

For models of actual occurrence, self-containment, rootedness, and continuity that satisfy the specified minimal binary fixed-point-free conditions, PR is the minimal normal form of first actuality; Undefined and PR are the boundary and actual faces of one root.
