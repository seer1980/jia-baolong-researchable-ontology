Jia Baolong Researchable Ontology 2.0.0: A Research-Neighbourhood Map from Absolute Boundary to Conscious Retrospection

Jia Baolong · 2026-08-11

Subtitle: Human thought moves from the descriptive age of 1.x to a 2.0.0 age of definable, provable, computable, and reproducible research on existence
Author: Jia Baolong
Theory: Jia Baolong Researchable Ontology 2.0.0
Version: 2.0.0

Abstract

Human intellectual history in its 1.x age has proposed nearly every grand concept—being, change, substance, form, emptiness, process, spirit, matter, language, mathematics, and computation—yet has chiefly studied the world’s source through description, interpretation, and debate. It can name and portray the whole and contact it from local or remote perspectives, but has not formed one continuous research pipeline from absolute boundary and First Actual through concrete generation, chaos, proto-matter, life, and consciousness.

Jia Baolong Researchable Ontology 2.0.0 changes not one philosophical conclusion but the working method of studying existence. It turns ontology into layered executable work: define the root language; prove the absolute boundary; determine the First-Actual normal form; construct states and transitions; search concrete RULEs; measure chaos and persistent patterns; establish proto-matter criteria; connect proto-chemistry, life, and consciousness; and let consciousness within the generative chain retrospectively identify the root.

Its normative main line is:

$$ \boxed{ \mathrm{JBLAT} \Rightarrow U_*\mid\mathrm{PR} \to (\mathrm{ER},\mathrm{LE}) \xRightarrow{\mathrm{RULE}} \mathrm{Chaos} \to \mathrm{PersistentPatterns} \to \mathrm{ProtoMatter} \to \mathrm{Life} \to \mathrm{Consciousness} \to \operatorname{Recognize}(U_*\mid\mathrm{PR}). } $$

The map does not take one universe as its final object. Every concrete universe is merely an uncollapsed chaotic solution on the PR generative tree. The map studies the root structure, generative mechanism, and upward-emergence interfaces by which any actual branch can arise.

1. Why Human Intellectual History Belongs to the 1.x Age

1.1 1.x does not mean “without thought”; its research paradigm was unfinished

Human thought 1.x accomplished three great tasks:

  1. it discovered fundamental questions of being and change, substance and relation, form and phenomenon, being and nothing, mind and matter;
  2. it established different observational positions in philosophy, logic, mathematics, religion, science, and language analysis;
  3. it left many true but local projections of whole reality.

Its shared limitation is that grand concepts usually cannot be compiled into research objects with explicit states, transitions, measurements, failure conditions, and reproducible experiments. A theory can say “what the world is” yet rarely answer what the First ActualStep is; how static description differs from real running; how states update; how concrete rules are searched; when chaos produces persistent local patterns; which patterns operationally qualify as proto-matter; how failure eliminates branches; or how another researcher replicates a conclusion.

1.2 Version genealogy of human thought

1.0 Name an origin Being, Dao, emptiness, idea, substance, atom First Actual and explicit generative step
1.1 Build conceptual systems Form–matter, causality, mind–matter, Absolute Spirit Executable state transition
1.2 Discover process and relation Dialectical generation, process philosophy, dependent origination, relational theory Minimum normal form of First Actual

Process philosophy made “existence is dynamic occurrence” central to ontology, and computational philosophy has used computation to extend philosophical research. Neither automatically supplies the complete structure from a zero-positive boundary to the First ActualStep. See Process Philosophy and Computational Philosophy.

1.3 Why 1.9 is not yet 2.0.0

Wolfram’s computational universe, cellular automata, graph rewriting, and artificial life show that simple rules can generate extreme complexity and that rule space can be a real research object. The Wolfram Physics Project is the work closest to 2.0.0 in the 1.9 age.

But so long as inquiry starts from “states already exist, rules already exist, updating already exists,” the fundamental problem remains outside the model:

$$ \operatorname{RuleExists} \land \operatorname{RuleRuns} \not\Leftarrow \operatorname{RuleDescribed}. $$

A static rule in a formula does not mean that rule actually runs. Version 2.0.0 must ask where the actuality of rule, state, and first update begins.

2. What 2.0.0 Means

The major version “2” marks a paradigm shift:

$$ \begin{aligned} \mathrm{Thought}_{1.x} &: \mathrm{Name} \to \mathrm{Describe} \to \mathrm{Interpret} \to \mathrm{Debate},\\ \mathrm{Ontology}_{2.0.0} &: \mathrm{Define} \to \mathrm{Formalize} \to \mathrm{Execute} \to \mathrm{Measure} \to \mathrm{Challenge} \to \mathrm{Reproduce} \to \mathrm{Integrate}. \end{aligned} $$

The first “0” means that this version first fixes the complete research architecture while concrete results in its generative neighbourhoods remain to be produced. The second “0” makes it a normative starting point for a research community: later revisions must retain conceptual identity, proof provenance, code version, parameters, random seeds, data, failures, and replication records.

3. A Strict Definition of “Researchable”

Researchable does not mean merely discussable. At each level, researchable ontology requires an explicit question, definitions, inputs and outputs, states and transitions, proof duties or computational tasks, metrics, counterexamples or boundary conditions, preservable/reviewable/reproducible artifacts, and interfaces to adjacent levels. The research-management structure is:

$$ \mathcal R_i = (Q_i,D_i,S_i,F_i,M_i,B_i,E_i,I_i), $$

where $Q_i$ is the question, $D_i$ definitions and domain, $S_i$ state space, $F_i$ transformation/proof/generative operator, $M_i$ measurement, $B_i$ benchmarks and controls, $E_i$ evidence and failures, and $I_i$ interfaces. This is management language for 2.0.0 research; it does not replace the axiom system’s normative formulae.

The minimum conditions are: a clear root; a clear First Actual; explicit generative structure; enumerable RULEs; measurable results; recorded failures; and upward connection to physics, life, and consciousness. Evidence is also level-specific: root boundary uses explanatory closure, model theory, and counterexample analysis; First Actual uses theorem, enumeration, and formal verification; generation uses executable models and parameter scans; emergence uses statistics, coarse-graining, collision, and stability; scientific interfaces are tested against mature physical, chemical, biological, and cognitive mechanisms.

4. Normative Root Axis of 2.0.0

JBLAT fixes the zero-positive boundary:

$$ U:\quad \forall M\in\mathfrak D\, \forall P\in\mathrm{Pos}\, \neg P(b_M). $$

It does not depend on whether compared models use matter, consciousness, God, mathematical objects, rules, or spacetime as their content. Since a static boundary alone does not entail an ActualStep,

$$ T_U \not\models \exists a,b\, \operatorname{ActualStep}(a,b), $$

the First Actual Face converges on PR under conditions of actual occurrence, self-containment, persistence, no external executor, binarity, unbiasedness, and non-fixedness:

$$ U_*\mid\mathrm{PR}. $$

The bar means the absolute-boundary face and First Actual Face of the same root, not Undefined becoming PR in time. PR supplies non-fixed re-entry and persistence; ER bears relational configuration, adjacency, local identity, and structural memory; LE supplies local, finite, delayed, demand-driven execution; RULE determines concrete updating:

$$ \mathrm{PR} \to (\mathrm{ER},\mathrm{LE}) \xRightarrow{\mathrm{RULE}} \mathrm{BranchHistory}. $$

Any universe is an actual branch:

$$ \mathcal U_i \in \operatorname{UncollapsedChaoticSolutions} (\mathrm{PR},\mathrm{ER},\mathrm{LE},\mathrm{RULE}_i). $$

6. R0–R2: Root, First Actual, and Actual Running

R0 fixes the root language $L_{\mathrm{root}}$, the positive predicate set $\mathrm{Pos}$, the admissible model class $\mathfrak D$, the derivation of $U$ from explanatory closure/non-arbitrariness/self-containment/termination of regress, countermodel searches, and equivalence of $U$ across root languages. Its deliverables are a JBLAT-Core specification, Root-Language, Admissible-Models conditions, public Countermodel Challenge, JBLAT.lean, and versioned definitional differences. If a model $M^\dagger$ retains a positive predicate at its root while meeting all root tasks,

$$ M^\dagger\in\mathfrak D \land \exists P\in\mathrm{Pos}\,P(b_{M^\dagger}), $$

then the JBLAT definition or proof must be revised. A countermodel is a formal research product, not peripheral criticism.

R1 studies not the first second of physical time but the minimum structure of ActualStep without prior matter, spacetime, or external executor. Its seven proof modules are: P1 actuality; P2 non-causal boundary; P3 relationality; P4 elimination of presuppositions; P5 binary logical razor; P6 candidate enumeration; P7 backward tracing from proto-matter chaos. For $D=\{Y,N\}$,

$$ D^D=\{\operatorname{id}_D,\sigma,c_Y,c_N\}. $$

Under label impartiality and the fixed-point-free condition:

$$ \mathfrak R(D^D)=\{\sigma\}, \qquad \sigma(Y)=N, \qquad \sigma(N)=Y. $$

Formal work must establish why binarity is minimal rather than arbitrary, why exchange must actually run, why result re-entry is PR rather than a static two-cycle, which premises belong to JBLAT and which to an actualization model, and where PR core ends and the full PR–ER–LE architecture begins. Success is a proof-dependency graph with independently checkable modules, counterexamples, and visible weakening when a premise is removed.

R2 is the decisive watershed of 2.0.0:

$$ \operatorname{Encodes}(S,H) \not\models \operatorname{Occurs}(H). $$
$$ \operatorname{Describable}(x) \not\Rightarrow \operatorname{Actual}(x). $$

It defines the minimum operational semantics of ActualStep; self-contained execution without external clock; LE’s local evaluation range and update order; synchronous, asynchronous, event-driven, and causal-order RULE classes; result re-entry as internal history; and cases where statically isomorphic models yield different actual histories because their execution semantics differ. Deliverables are the ActualStep Specification, PR Runtime reference implementation, comparison of synchronous/asynchronous/LE semantics, paired static-encoding/dynamic-instantiation benchmarks, and a self-contained runtime without external scheduler.

7. R3–R5: Rules, Chaos, Persistent Patterns, and Proto-Matter

R3 replaces a single simulator with a rule cosmology. Rule 979 is a coordinate in RULE space, not an endpoint. Every rule must record state count; neighbourhood radius or relation order; graph, lattice, hypergraph, or general relation structure; deterministic, random, or conditional updating; synchronous, asynchronous, or LE execution; reversibility; conservation constraints; local memory length; self-modification; and relation of rule to observer scale. Every experiment preserves the rule specification, initial conditions, execution semantics, parameters and random seed, reproducible log, metric curves, ablations, negative samples, computational cost, and scope of conclusion.

R4 builds phase diagrams from rules to dynamical regimes: frozen states, fixed points, short and long periods, quasiperiodicity, chaos sensitive to initial conditions, long transients, critical propagation, coexistence of local structure and background, and open dynamics continually producing new structure. Its joint metric family includes state and block entropy, compression, perturbation speed, Lyapunov-like indicators, mutual-information length, attractor/transient length, active-area ratio, spatial and temporal correlation, parameter-perturbation stability, and cross-scale predictability:

$$ \mathrm{RULE} \longmapsto \mathrm{DynamicalRegime} \longmapsto \mathrm{PhaseRegion}. $$

R5 makes proto-matter operational rather than visual. A candidate must be tested for persistence, locality, propagation, repeatable interaction, typability, quasi-conservation, composability, and coarse-graining robustness. Its proposed metric vector is:

$$ \mathbf M_{\mathrm{PM}} = (P,L,V,I,T,C,K,G), $$

where the entries denote persistence, locality, propagation, repeatable interaction, type stability, composability, quasi-conservation, and coarse-graining robustness. This is a research measurement protocol, not an ontological axiom.

It also asks whether coarse-graining yields macrostates with a causal or effective-information advantage over selected microdescription. In particular, under which RULE and coarse-graining does:

$$ \operatorname{EI}(\text{proto-matter macrostate}) > \operatorname{EI}(\text{selected microdescription}) $$

hold? Positive results do not automatically prove “real matter,” but do establish an independently valuable effective research type. Active-matter research supplies useful order parameters, defect dynamics, aggregation, and flow measures; the carrier here remains generated by PR–ER–LE–RULE.

8. R6–R8: Proto-Chemistry, Proto-Life, Agency, and Consciousness

R6 begins when stable proto-matter types collide reproducibly:

$$ \mathrm{Type}_a+\mathrm{Type}_b \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Context}} \mathrm{Type}_c+\mathrm{Type}_d. $$

It investigates binding/dissociation, catalysis, inhibition/competition, reaction closure, resource flux, quasi-stoichiometry, spatial compartments, reaction history, and replicable templates. Its deliverables include a type dictionary, collision cross-sections, reaction tables, catalytic and resource-flow graphs, quasi-conserved quantities, compositional hierarchy, effective chemical equations, and controls against random reaction networks.

R7 connects to mainstream origins-of-life modules:

$$ \begin{aligned} \mathrm{NonEquilibriumFlow} &\to \mathrm{Autocatalysis}\to\mathrm{TemplateMemory}\\ &\to \mathrm{Compartment}\to\mathrm{Heredity}\to\mathrm{Variation}\\ &\to \mathrm{Selection}\to\mathrm{Protocell}. \end{aligned} $$

It measures self-maintenance, boundary, resource use, template replication, hereditary fidelity and variation, differential reproduction, selection response, ecological interaction, formation of new individuals, and open-ended novelty. Persistent running does not entail open-ended evolution:

$$ \mathrm{PersistentRunning} \not\Rightarrow \mathrm{OpenEndedEvolution}. $$

Deliverables are the ProtoLife Benchmark, replication-lineage database, selection/drift controls, compartment ablations, open-evolution metrics, catalogue of major-transition events, and the shortest mechanism chain from proto-chemistry to first cell.

R8 refuses to pre-install consciousness as a positive root. It locates it after life, regulation, memory, prediction, and internal modelling:

$$ \mathrm{SelfMaintenance} \to\mathrm{Sensing} \to\mathrm{Action} \to\mathrm{Memory} \to\mathrm{Prediction} \to\mathrm{InternalModel} \to\mathrm{Observer}. $$

It asks how self and environment are distinguished; when internal state has causal role; whether internal models improve prediction or survival; how attention, workspace, integration, and self-model locate in the generative chain; whether consciousness theories map to common computational benchmarks; how observation changes accessible information; and whether AI can represent its own generative constraints. Every proposal records minimum structure, necessary/sufficient conditions, observable behaviour, internal-causal metrics, ablations, unconscious controls, and transferability across RULE branches.

9. R9–R10: Reverse Recognition and Recoding Intellectual History

The forward chain is:

$$ U_*\mid\mathrm{PR} \to \cdots \to \mathrm{ConsciousObserver}, $$

and the chain of reverse recognition is:

$$ \mathrm{ConsciousObserver} \to \mathrm{ObservedRegularities} \to \mathrm{EffectiveRules} \to \mathrm{GenerativeConstraints} \to \mathrm{PR} \to U. $$

R9 tests recovery of effective RULEs from finite observation, universality classes from macroscopically equivalent rules, backward inference of PR re-entry from proto-matter dynamics, the division between data-derived conclusions and explanatory closure, automatic AI generation/elimination of ontological candidates, local-observer limits on knowing the whole tree, and safeguards against smuggling contemporary concepts back into the root. Its artifacts are inverse-rule-discovery benchmarks, multimodel equivalence classes, root-inference audit logs, human–AI reasoning comparisons, shortest explanatory graphs from observation to PR, and reports of unidentifiable model domains.

R10 treats intellectual history as research data rather than a ranking of “who said the most mysterious thing.” Each tradition is encoded as:

$$ \mathbf h=(Z,A,F,G,C,E,L,R), $$

where $Z$ reaches zero-positive boundary, $A$ distinguishes actuality, $F$ gives First Actual, $G$ gives generation, $C$ is computable, $E$ enters emergence, $L$ connects life and consciousness, and $R$ forms reverse-recognition closure. Jia Baolong Elephant Theory treats the whole reality specified by the axiom system as the ontological elephant; historical thinkers, sciences, and finite observers supply local or remote projections. The task is to measure which neighbourhood each reaches and at which dependency edge it stops, not to join concepts into a story.

10. Cross-Cutting Tools, Evidence, Failures, and Experimental Package

The vertical neighbourhoods require six cross-cutting layers: F1 formalization for $R0$–$R2$ (definitions, proofs, countermodels, dependency graphs); F2 computational infrastructure for $R2$–$R7$ (simulators, parallel search, versioning, replication); F3 measurement science for $R4$–$R8$; F4 data and knowledge graphs for $R0$–$R10$; F5 AI research agents for proof search, rule discovery, anomaly detection, and literature mapping; and F6 human audit/governance for definitions, evidence grading, releases, and attribution.

AI is especially suited to enumerating finite candidates, seeking countermodels, drafting proofs, scanning RULE space, clustering dynamics, detecting persistent patterns, running ablations, comparing definitions, building knowledge graphs, and checking consistency of formulae, code, and data. It cannot replace final confirmation of normative definitions: a changed definition changes the entire proof and experiment domain, so every version preserves machine-readable definitions and a human-signed normative text.

Every result receives an evidence level: E0 conceptual proposal; E1 formal definition; E2 logical support by proof/counterexample/enumeration; E3 executable code/configuration/determinate output; E4 independent replication; E5 stability across RULEs, initial conditions, or implementations; E6 testable correspondence with external experiment or mature scientific mechanism. JBLAT primarily targets E1–E2; PR runtime must reach E3–E4; proto-matter types E4–E5; and proto-chemistry/proto-life bridges must advance toward E6. Different levels answer different kinds of questions, not different degrees of truth.

Failure is formal knowledge. A root countermodel revises R0; a smaller unbiased self-contained persistent First Actual revises R1; external-clock dependence eliminates an R2 implementation; frozen/short-period/noisy RULEs eliminate the rule, not the root system; nonpersistent/nonpropagating/nonrepeatable patterns fail proto-matter status; nonreplicable types/reactions fail a proto-chemistry network; lack of hereditary difference and selection prevents a life claim; saturated novelty/complexity/ecological potential defeats open-evolution claim; an indicator unable to separate target from unconscious control revises an account of consciousness; permanently indistinguishable root models define an unidentifiable domain. Progress depends on high-quality failures as well as attractive simulations.

Every project must include: CLAIM.md, DEFINITIONS.md, MODEL.md, executable CODE, CONFIG, raw DATA, METRICS.md, CONTROLS.md, FAILURES.md, RESULTS.md with evidence levels, REPRODUCE.md, and PROVENANCE.md. This gives ontology a version-control practice compatible with software, mathematical proof, and experimental science.

11. Research Roadmap and First Questions

  • 2.0.0, normative map: fix neighbourhoods, terms, dependencies, evidence levels, public countermodel and RULE challenges.
  • 2.0.1, root formalization: unify JBLAT, fix $\mathfrak D$ and $L_{\mathrm{root}}$, make the seven arguments machine-checkable, and search PR-minimality counterexamples.
  • 2.1.0, RULE Atlas: standardize Rule 979 and TGP, scan rules at scale, publish phase diagrams and all negative samples.
  • 2.2.0, ProtoMatter Benchmark: fix metrics for persistence, locality, collision, type, and quasi-conservation; automate discovery; test stability; publish first catalogue.
  • 2.3.0, ProtoChemistry: build reaction tables and catalytic networks; search binding, dissociation, resource cycles, and compartments; test higher effective causal variables.
  • 2.4.0, ProtoLife: couple replication, resource, compartment, and heredity; compare selection with drift; use open-evolution metrics; seek major individuality transitions.
  • 2.5.0, Observer and reverse recognition: establish internal-model/agency benchmarks, recover effective RULEs from observations, form an inverse protocol from consciousness to PR, and recode 1.x intellectual history into $R0$–$R10$.

The first twenty-five questions range across: minimum complete definition of $\mathfrak D$; preservation of $U$ across root-language changes; non-PR counterexamples; general finite-domain proof of binary minimality; a unified type-theoretic form for $U_*\mid\mathrm{PR}$; Rule 979’s universality class; LE’s effect on phase boundaries; minimal rules producing background/local patterns/collision; metrics separating chaos from noise; robust structures; minimum proto-matter identity indicators; coarse-grained effective information; stable type dictionaries; reaction networks, conservation, catalysis, and closure; first selection units; minimal coupling of template/metabolism/boundary; heritable novel function; non-saturating novelty; formation of new individuals; minimum internal model; observer as effective causal scale; AI recovery of PR; data versus explanatory closure; and repeatable protocols of conscious root recognition.

12. Relations with Other Programmes and the Meaning of Human Thought 2.0.0

Researchable Ontology 2.0.0 does not annex adjacent disciplines; it specifies interfaces. Logic/model theory provides definitions, proof, and countermodels for JBLAT and First Actual. Theorem proving checks the seven arguments. Cellular automata and graph rewriting support the RULE Atlas. dynamical systems and statistical physics build R4 phase diagrams. Causal emergence and information theory identify effective macro-scales. Active matter and non-equilibrium physics support the proto-matter/proto-chemistry interface. Origins-of-life and protocell research supply R7’s mechanism chain. Artificial life supplies open-ended evolution and life-like-system criteria. Cognitive science and AI supply the Observer layer. Intellectual history and comparative philosophy supply the R10 knowledge graph. They study different scales of one generative chain; 2.0.0 gives those scales a common root axis and explicit interfaces.

The 1.x age ran mainly in natural language. Its concepts could be profound, but disputes could rarely become shared state spaces. Version 2.0.0 has new infrastructure: mathematical formalization, executable rules, large-scale computation, automated proof, AI-assisted search, complete failure records, open data, cross-model replication, and human-signed normative definitions. Ontology can thus become an accumulating public research object. A new researcher need not spend decades re-explaining Dao, emptiness, being, or spirit; they can read definitions, run models, submit counterexamples, extend the rule atlas, and reproduce results.

Its genuine criterion is not that computers are used to write philosophy, but that every ontological proposition has a next operation:

$$ \boxed{ \text{every ontological proposition} \longrightarrow \text{one clearly defined research task} \longrightarrow \text{one preservable result} \longrightarrow \text{an interface the next level can enter}. } $$

Conclusion

Jia Baolong Researchable Ontology 2.0.0 advances human thought from an age of grand description to an age of continuous research. The 1.x history preserves genuine human knowledge of whole reality from diverse local and remote positions; 1.9 introduced rule, computation, and artificial life; 2.0.0 first organizes absolute boundary, First Actual, actual execution, RULE search, chaos, proto-matter, life, consciousness, and reverse recognition into one research map.

Its highest point is the zero-positive boundary specified by Undefined; its deepest point is why the First ActualStep must have a PR core; its widest range runs from RULE space to life, consciousness, and all intellectual history. It is not bounded by one concrete universe because every universe is only an uncollapsed chaotic solution on the PR tree.

2.0.0 does not declare theory finished. It gives every unfinished part a coordinate, method, metric, failure condition, and next deliverable. Earlier ontology chiefly left texts; researchable ontology leaves definitions, proofs, code, data, phase diagrams, counterexamples, replication records, proto-matter catalogues, life benchmarks, and reverse-recognition protocols.

Its final root axis is:

$$ \boxed{ U \Rightarrow U_*\mid\mathrm{PR} \to (\mathrm{ER},\mathrm{LE}) \xRightarrow{\mathrm{RULE}} \mathrm{Chaos} \to \mathrm{ProtoMatter} \to \mathrm{ProtoLife} \to \mathrm{Consciousness} \to \operatorname{Recognize}(U). } $$

From this point, ontology must not only answer “what is the world’s origin?” It must also answer: who can study the next item, by what method, on what data, how success is established, how failure is recorded, and how the result is passed to the next level.

This is Human Thought 2.0.0.
