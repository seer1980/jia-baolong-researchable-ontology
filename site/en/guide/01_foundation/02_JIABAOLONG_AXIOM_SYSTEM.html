Focus I: The Jia Baolong Axiom System

Jia Baolong

The Historical Two-Axiom Formulation

First Axiom: Dual Paradigms and the Placeholder Boundary

Early axiomatic texts divided the ultimate level into:

  1. Undefined: a placeholder boundary carrying no positive properties.
  2. Platonic Crystal: static mathematical possibilities and complete formal structures.
  3. The PR generative domain: the domain of actual dynamic generation in which universes are investigated.

The central value of this distinction is not the claim that these three are entities of the same kind. It prevents three conceptual confusions:

\[ \text{describable} \neq \text{actually occurring}, \qquad \text{static completeness} \neq \text{dynamic generation}. \]

Second Axiom: The PR–ER–LE Triadic Generative Mechanism

The early formulation described the three as an inseparable generative loop:

\[ \mathrm{PR}\Longleftrightarrow\mathrm{ER}\Longleftrightarrow\mathrm{LE}. \]
  • PR supplies non-fixedness, persistent difference, and re-entry.
  • ER supplies relational situations, structural memory, and local identity.
  • LE supplies local unfolding, truncation, delay, and finite processing.

The Current Rigorous Interpretation

Later papers tightened the two axioms in four important ways.

Tightening One: Undefined Is a Boundary

Undefined is the root boundary of zero positive determination; PR is its actual face:

\[ \mathrm{Undefined} \not\models \operatorname{Actuality}. \]

Undefined and PR are therefore not two successive objects in a temporal causal chain. They are the boundary face and the actual face of one and the same root.

Tightening Two: Actuality Is Motion

The system investigates generative universes in which actual occurrence takes place. The identity of existence and motion is the ontological choice of domain:

\[ \operatorname{ActualExistence} \equiv_{\text{framework}} \operatorname{ActualMotion}. \]

This is the system’s definition of existence.

Tightening Three: PR Is the Minimal Normal Form

Under conditions of a binary domain, total functions, unbiasedness / label symmetry, persistence, and non-fixedness, the only map left is the swap:

\[ \mathfrak R(D^D)=\{\sigma\}. \]

Actuality is supplied by a genuinely executed step:

\[ \operatorname{ActualStep}_W(x,y) \land y=\sigma(x). \]

Tightening Four: RULE Determines Emergence

The two-cycle of PR itself provides the First Motive. ER, LE, and specific RULEs determine local updating, state space, memory, truncation, and branching; they can subsequently form chaos, proto-matter, life, and consciousness.

The Minimal Modern Form of the Axiom System

The current system can be reconstructed in four layers:

  1. Boundary layer: models that complete the explanatory task without positive presuppositions have roots satisfying zero positive determination.
  2. Actuality layer: the domain selects actual occurrence rather than static encoding.
  3. First-Beat layer: under explicit S0–S7, or equivalent, conditions, the minimal binary normal form without a fixed point on the actual face is PR.
  4. Universe-generation layer: PR, ER, LE, and specific RULEs jointly determine whether chaos, structure, proto-matter, life, and consciousness emerge.
