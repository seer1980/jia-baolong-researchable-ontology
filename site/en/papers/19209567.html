The Essence of the Universe: A Medium-Free Fractal Chaotic System Based on Self-Referential Paradox

Jia Baolong · 2026-03-25 · Zenodo record · DOI

Authors: Jia Baolong (Jia, Baolong) \quad Niu Quanfu$^*$
Independent Researchers
Email: seer@139.com

Abstract

This paper proposes a new ontological model of the universe. It holds that the universe does not run on any physical medium, but is a medium-free fractal chaotic system driven by the triad of Self-Reference, Entity–Relation, and Lazy Evaluation. Through a contrapositive derivation, the paper argues that “paradox” is not a logical defect but an absolute element of the universe’s existence and evolution. It compares the model with Wolfram’s computational-universe theory, emphasizes the central place of self-reference in medium-free computation, and advances five core conjectures concerning nested universe models and limits of machine-code analysis (the Jia Baolong conjectures). Our physical universe is only one particular solution in this vast fractal-chaotic system of pure logic.

Translator’s note: The Chinese abstract says “five core conjectures,” while Section 5 of the same source actually presents ten. The translation preserves the abstract’s wording and records the internal discrepancy rather than silently correcting it.

Solemn statement: This paper is a purely theoretical exploration based on Gödel’s incompleteness theorems and first principles. Because it touches the lowest layer of logic, the theory cannot be directly falsified in the conventional physical sense. The authors sincerely welcome and expect rigorous criticism and examination from physics and computer science.

1. Introduction: paradox and I

As a child, I was deeply fascinated by the barber paradox and always tried to find a perfect solution. At that time, the world’s imperfection and apparent loopholes in logic troubled me. Later I encountered the library paradox and Russell’s paradox; intuition told me strongly that these apparently different intellectual games were saying the same thing.

When I later studied logic in depth, one startling idea struck me. By the contrapositive: if “where there is a world, there must be paradox,” then “without paradox, there is no world.” For someone obsessed with an ultimate “perfect world”—later I learned that this is philosophically connected with the ideal of Occam’s razor—I had once regarded paradox as a bug a system needed to repair. I eventually realized that paradox is far from being a defect; it is precisely an absolute element that constitutes this world.

Later still, I read Gödel’s incompleteness theorems [1]. Again intuition told me that this was still the same matter as Russell’s paradox. Remove the complex mathematical clothing and there is only one essence: self-referential paradox. Russell’s paradox, Gödelian incompleteness, and even the origin of the universe, I concluded, are one thing.

2. An ontology of the universe: medium-free computation and triadic dynamics

From this recognition of self-referential paradox, I advance a central claim about the nature of the universe: the universe is a vast fractal chaotic system, and the physical universe we observe is only one “solution” within it.

This fractal system runs not on physical hardware such as silicon or qubits, but evolves through pure logical medium-free computation [2]. In a Buddhist analogy, “form is emptiness”: macroscopic matter is, in essence, only topological lines entangled in logical emptiness.

Before examining the concrete operation of this fractal system, we must make a philosophical distinction between the universe’s ultimate ontology and its generative mechanism.

At the highest level of ultimate ontology, the universe is absolutely monistic. The root of all things is only the pure logical verb “Self-Reference.” It is the universe’s sole heart and engine; beyond it there is nothing else.

Yet stopping at monism would leave a theory that is philosophically pure but unable to render the macroscopic physical world before us. An engine, however strong, would spin in the void without gears and tracks. To discuss the generative mechanism through which a “logical singularity” evolves into all things, two further concepts must be introduced: Entity–Relation (ER) and Lazy Evaluation (LE).

ER and LE are not externally imposed rules. They are the fractal traces and modes of handling that self-reference necessarily leaves in the void as it attempts to resolve its own paradox. Monism—self-reference—is the soul of the universe; ER and LE are the skeleton and metabolism that this soul necessarily develops as it oscillates.

These three lowest-level logical pieces drive the system’s evolution [3]. A biological analogy, which also resonates with the Daoist line “Dao gives birth to one, one gives birth to two, two gives birth to three, and three gives birth to the myriad things,” may state them as follows:

  1. Self-Reference (SR)—the universe’s “gene” (Dao gives birth to one): Self-reference is the origin of everything and the first driving force of the universe. Like a gene containing instructions for self-replication, self-referential paradox prevents a logical system from ever becoming stationary, producing perpetual oscillation and evolution.
  2. Entity–Relation (ER)—the universe’s “body structure” (one gives birth to two): Entities and relations weave a topological network. No presupposed three-dimensional space is needed; space itself is a geometric structure woven from countless ER links.
  3. Lazy Evaluation (LE)—the universe’s “mode of life activity” (two gives birth to three): The system does not calculate everything globally and synchronously. It processes on demand the logical conflicts produced by self-reference. This local, asynchronous computation appears macroscopically as computational friction, gravity, and the passage of time.

3. Seeking the rules by which God plays (the ultimate razor)

If self-reference, entity–relation, and lazy evaluation are the three known pieces, how does “God” play with them?

Out of a commitment to a perfect world—the fewest rules under Occam’s razor—the rules must be simple enough that no arbitrary constant is permitted (even a simple “multiply by three”), and no patch-like explanation may be allowed.

I do not yet know the concrete rules completely. But I have found two highly elegant possible solutions and offer them as a starting point, sincerely inviting scientists around the world to investigate them further.

3.1 Solution one: the triad plus interaction combinators (the Lafont model)

Yves Lafont’s interaction combinators [4] were not originally designed for cosmology. After adaptation, however, they can fit Trinity Theory. In this solution, the rule is reduced to an extreme: only when nodes meet face to face does pure graph-theoretic geometric reorganization occur—elimination, annihilation, or duplicated weaving. With no constants, the encounter of SR (replicating nodes) and ER (constructing nodes) alone can burst an infinitely complex topological network out of a medium-free void. Preliminary code simulations indicate that this scheme can naturally generate highly fractal structures and deterministic-chaotic properties.

3.2 Solution two: the triad plus topological doubling

This is an even more elementary proposal. Imagine the simplest topology: a single closed loop. The only rule is to stretch the loop to twice its length, then wind and map it back onto itself. Such an extremely simple self-referential mapping would instantly produce extreme topological mixing and butterfly effects in mathematics, generating topological chaos and fractal dust.

4. Comparison with Wolfram’s computational-universe theory

Any discussion of computational universes must mention Stephen Wolfram. When my theory of the “Logical Universe” was published, I knew and liked WolframAlpha, the excellent website, but did not yet know Wolfram’s own large computational-universe theory [5]. I express deep respect for this pioneer.

Compared with Wolfram’s theory, my theory has a tendency toward “pure perfection,” apart from its insistence on paradox:

  • No presuppositions: Wolfram’s model still depends on a presupposed update rule and discrete grid or hypergraph evolution steps. My theory requires no presupposed “thing”; it is built entirely on the void.
  • The central place of self-reference: This is the largest feature of my theory. Wolfram’s systems evolve forward, whereas mine takes self-referential paradox as the universe’s sole engine. Without self-reference, there is neither quantum superposition nor the awakening of consciousness.

5. Ten Jia Baolong conjectures

On the basis of medium-free computation and Trinity Theory, I propose ten core conjectures about the nature of the universe and computation.

  • Conjecture 1: a nested model of the medium-free computational universe and computational universes. The universe has a nested structure. At the lowest level stands the medium-free computational universe studied here—pure logic. Above it emerge and layer computational universes based on physical media. The “universes of game worlds” created by humans, if they qualify, are a higher-level example in this nested model.

  • Conjecture 2: is our universe medium-free, or does it have a computer? Is our universe directly at the bottom medium-free level, or does it run on a “physical computer” (a parent universe) that emerged from lower-level logic? This is an ultimate question still requiring proof.

  • Conjecture 3: scientists within a computational universe cannot understand all its machine code completely (100%). If our universe is a computer, scientists inside it can never parse its underlying machine code completely. Observation is itself a computational operation and therefore necessarily produces a Gödelian blind spot.

  • Conjecture 4: in a computational universe, lower-universe machine code cannot be studied. Observers in the present universe cannot penetrate a “virtual-machine barrier” to study the machine code or hardware of the lower, parent universe that runs the present one.

  • Conjecture 5: no paradox-free universe exists. No logically absolutely self-consistent universe without paradox exists. A system lacking self-referential paradox is stagnant water and can never generate chaotic evolution, life, or consciousness.

  • Conjecture 6: absolute nothingness is intrinsically unstable. In absolute nothingness without any entity or relation, quantum fluctuation or the necessity of logical self-reference prevents stability; topological structure must arise from nothing.

  • Conjecture 7: spacetime is an emergent appearance of Entity–Relation links and lazy computation. The physical space and time we perceive are not the universe’s underlying background. They are an emergent appearance produced macroscopically by the topological connections of ER and the asynchronous processing of LE.

  • Conjecture 8: physical constants are discrete processing limits of the logical universe. Fundamental constants such as the speed of light and Planck’s constant are not values arbitrarily set by God. They are limiting bottlenecks encountered when a pure logical graph-network transmits discrete information and performs computation.

  • Conjecture 9: consciousness is a high-order structural projection of the universe’s self-reference engine. Human consciousness is not merely a biological miracle. It is a high-order topological projection of the universe’s bottom-level SR engine after innumerable fractal iterations in a macroscopically complex network.

  • Conjecture 10: the universe’s final heat death is asymptotically impossible. Because self-referential paradox drives the system eternally, the system can never attain an absolute equilibrium. The final heat death predicted by conventional thermodynamics is never reached in the infinite asymptotic sense.

6. Conclusion

The universe is not “computed” by some supercomputer. The universe itself is a process of pure logical computation. At the deepest layer of all things there are no strings, membranes, or quantum foam. There are only pure logical relations and a self-referential paradox that can never prove itself true.

Paradox—the logical blemish that once troubled me—is the only reason the universe can exist, breathe, and evolve into you and me.
