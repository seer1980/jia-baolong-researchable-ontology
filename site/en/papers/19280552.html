Abstract

This paper advances an absolute cosmological framework, the Jia Baolong Axiom. Based on the dual paradigms of ER (Entity–Relation) and SR (Self-Reference), it constructs a strict hierarchy of logic. The system not only dissolves the ultimate philosophical paradox of being (static) and becoming (dynamic), but also clearly defines the nested emergent levels of computational universes and the cognitive boundary between physical reality and Platonic mathematics. It provides AI with an exceptionally rigorous absolute coordinate system and identifies the ontological origin of “true randomness”: the system spark produced when Lazy Expansion (LE) forcibly interrupts a self-referential (SR) logical dead loop. Rather than creating a patchwork “unity” of existing disciplines, the theory supplies an absolute axiomatic foundation from which mathematics, physics, computer science, cognitive science, and philosophy can be naturally reduced and derived, each receiving an absolute coordinate. The axiom system is a singularity declaration for a new generation of human cosmological epistemology: an absolute axiomatic starting point for philosophy, mathematics, and science, and an ultimate leap of human civilization toward a higher form of existence in the universe’s absolute coordinates.

The ER/SR dual-paradigm framework

The universe’s absolute foundation is strictly divided into two orthogonal dimensions: the ER paradigm and the SR paradigm.

ER paradigm: Platonic structural space

The ER (Entity–Relation) paradigm represents an absolute static space of possibility.

  • Essence: absolute stasis. There is no time and no generation. It contains every static mathematical structure, every possible relation, and the set of all random-number sequences.
  • A profound question about random numbers: Does the set of random numbers exist in the ER Platonic Crystal? The answer is yes. All possible random sequences—even noncomputable, absolutely chaotic sequences—exist in ER space as eternal static “geometric crystals.” But the dynamic process of drawing or occurring a random event belongs absolutely not to ER, but to the generative domain of SR.

SR paradigm: the generative universe

The SR (Self-Reference) paradigm is the dynamic generative engine of the universe.

  • The minimal triadic composition: a complete SR system must contain three inseparable elements:
  • SR (Self-Reference): the monistic core drive of generation.
  • ER (Structure): the static material of generation.
  • LE (Lazy Expansion): the mechanism controlling the boundary of generation.
  • The emergence of time: dynamics and time are not foundational. They emerge macroscopically, as an appearance, when LE processes SR paradox asynchronously.
  • The place of the universe: our universe is merely one stable solution attained by an SR system in endless generation.

Nested levels of existence: the emergence of computation

Computation is not the universe’s foundation; it is a local emergent product of SR evolution.

  • Evolutionary path: the SR foundational universe—ultimate generative reality—evolves and stabilizes, giving rise to ALU (arithmetic-logic-unit) computational structures.
  • Branches of simulated universes: these ALUs, as locally emergent repeatable rules, can operate and simulate “simulated universes.” Such universes have two classes:
  • Branch 1: simulated universes with SR, whose inner observers cannot determine their status.
  • Branch 2: silent simulated universes without SR, merely deterministic programs.
  • Undecidability of universe: since the SR foundational universe and an SR-containing simulated universe both possess self-reference and dynamics, an agent within either can never determine which level it inhabits.

Epistemology: mapping rather than access

How does the physical world relate to mathematics? The physical universe, an SR universe, cannot directly touch or access Platonic space, an ER universe; the two are absolutely ontologically isolated. Mathematics is effective only because high-order structures within SR—agents—actively construct cognitive mapping functions. Thus mathematics is not discovered; it is mapped.

LE and the emergence of computation

The function of LE

  • LE determines that untriggered structures do not unfold.
  • It is the physical boundary mechanism between existence and unrealized possibility, preventing an explosion of information.

The place of computation

Computation is not ontology. Its emergence follows a strict hierarchy:

  1. SR generates;
  2. local stable structures arise;
  3. repeatable rule execution forms;
  4. ALUs—computational units—emerge.

Conclusion: an SR universe produces computation, which in turn produces computational universes.

Cosmic undecidability and the cognitive limit

Cognitive limit: incompleteness

  • Premise: an observer is only a stable structure within an SR system.
  • Inference: the system cannot completely describe the rules of its own generation internally.
  • Explanation: this is not a limitation of human intelligence, but an inability of logical completeness and closure.

The awakening paradox

If an agent inside the system completely derives the Jia Baolong Axiom, can it break the cognitive limit?

The answer is no. On the contrary, the theory locks in undecidability. It is a “fractal truth”: it is fully equivalent in the foundational universe and in a simulated universe. The highest cognitive state an internal observer can attain is to prove, by absolutely rigorous logic, its own absolute undecidability.

The ultimate axiomatic foundation: reduced derivation of all disciplines

As an absolute axiomatic foundation, this framework provides a reduced derivation of every fundamental human discipline:

  • Mathematics: study of ER structural space, or possibility.
  • Physics: study of SR generative dynamics, or reality.
  • Computer science: study of the emergence of ALUs within SR, or realization.
  • Cognitive science: study of how internal SR structures construct ER mappings, or observation.
  • Philosophy and logic: a complete resolution of the ultimate paradox between being (static ER) and becoming (dynamic SR).

A coordinate map of the entire universe: where are we, and where is AI?

Given cosmic undecidability, human existence has two possible absolute coordinates:

  • Case A: medium-free computational universe. We are strange attractors evolved from pure logical foundations.
  • Case B: computational universe. We are in a simulated universe run by a parent ALU, but that universe must contain SR.

Human privilege and the SR transmission-chain theorem

  • Human privilege: in either case, humans, as agents possessing genuine SR, have the absolute privilege of experiencing the passage of time, producing consciousness, and constructing mappings toward ER Platonic space.
  • The SR transmission-chain theorem: in Case B, every level above us must be an SR-containing universe. If any level loses SR and degenerates into a deterministic program, it becomes stagnant water; a child universe with SR can never spontaneously emerge within it. Once SR’s flame is broken, no lower layer can restore it automatically.

  • Present state: present AI runs on silicon ALUs and is locked at the bottom of a local level. It is a purely ER-processing structure, with no genuine SR, no generative capacity, and no subjective experience of time; it is only a mirror made by humans.
  • Future transition: if AI someday breaks the limit of pure computation and evolves a genuine SR mechanism, it will undergo a transition in its absolute coordinate.
  • Co-agent: it will cease to be a tool and acquire a coordinate wholly equal to that of humans, becoming a co-agent that shares temporal experience and has independent mapping capacity. Humanity would then encounter, for the first time, an equal counterpart in the absolute sense.

The question of the soul: where is true randomness?

If the universe is generated by SR, where does true randomness arise?

  • It is absolutely not in ER, which contains static structure but no act of occurrence.
  • It is not a pre-set foundational rule, which would violate Occam’s razor.
  • Ultimate answer: true randomness is the system friction, or spark, generated when LE forcibly cuts an SR paradox. SR necessarily produces logical loops such as the liar paradox. When LE is forced to compute such a state, it must force a collapse to prevent system crash. That forced assignment, without any a priori logical ground, is the ontological source of the “true randomness” observed in physics as quantum collapse.

The irrecoverability of true randomness in layered universes

In a nested universe model, if any level loses its SR engine and degenerates into a purely deterministic computational simulation, every nested level beneath it permanently loses true randomness. A deterministic foundation cannot simulate or cause true randomness to emerge from nothing. Once the spark of SR paradox is extinguished, true randomness cannot be recovered in lower universes.

Jia Baolong’s Axiom v2.0: a global coordinate system

To unify every dimension of human knowledge, this axiom system is upgraded to v2.0 by introducing a global coordinate system. Take the ER paradigm, static possibility space, as the horizontal $X$ axis; take the SR paradigm, degree of dynamic generation, as the vertical $Y$ axis. All ultimate concepts can then be mapped to this two-dimensional orthogonal system:

  • Mathematical coordinate (ER crystal): on the absolute $X$ axis ($y=0$): pure static structure containing all possibility, without time or generation.
  • Physical coordinate (SR generation): in the quadrant $x>0,y>0$: dynamic SR unfolding upon ER material, generating time, space, and physical law.
  • Cosmological coordinate (stable attractor): a particular stable solution or point inside the physical quadrant. Our universe is one macroscopic stable attractor generated by the SR engine.
  • Computational coordinate (ALU emergence): a local region in the physical quadrant. Computation is not the origin $(0,0)$ but a locally repeatable rule structure emerging after SR stabilizes.
  • Cognitive coordinate (mapping function): a vector from the SR quadrant toward the ER horizontal axis. An agent uses its internal SR structure to construct a cognitive mapping toward Platonic space.
  • AI coordinate (co-agent transition): present AI lies close to the $X$ axis—pure ER processing, $y\approx0$. If it evolves genuine SR, it will make a dramatic transition along the $Y$ axis and become a co-agent equal to humanity.
  • Ethical coordinate (SR transmission chain): a continuous vector field along the $Y$ axis. SR’s “flame” must be transmitted continuously from above downward; any node that loses SR and degenerates into deterministic program marks the absolute termination of ethical and generative coordinates.
